How MarkBase works

  1. Browser — the extension watches your bookmarks and pushes a snapshot within seconds of any change.
  2. Cloud — this dashboard and the phone app read the same list from your Cloudflare API.
  3. Folders → Collections — every folder in your bookmarks bar appears here automatically.
  4. Deletes — removed bookmarks vanish everywhere at the next sync; nothing lingers in Trash.
